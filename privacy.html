LastUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

LastUp is an alarm app that helps you go to bed on time and get up on time, with a small group of friends who can see when you're up. This policy explains what LastUp stores, why, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • Your alarm, bedtime and sleep history stay on your phone.
  • Only if you use the Group feature does LastUp store a name, a picture and your morning status online, so your group can see them.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my account.

What stays on your phone

Your alarm time and days, bedtime and wind-down reminder, wake-up task, alarm sound, chosen mascot and its name, and your sleep history (bedtimes, wake times, snoozes). This never leaves your phone.

What is stored online (only if you use Group)

DataWhy
A random account ID (no email, phone number or password)To keep you in your group

Only members of your group can see your name, picture, wake time and morning status. If you upload your own photo, it is stored at a long, hard-to-guess web address so it can be shown in the app; anyone who had that exact address could open it.

What LastUp does not collect

No location, contacts, microphone, camera recordings, advertising ID or browsing data. LastUp asks for notifications, exact alarms and to run in the background only so your alarm and bedtime reminder ring on time. If you choose your own photo, Android's photo picker shares just that one picture with the app.

Where it's stored and who processes it

Online data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage), hosted in Mumbai, India, and is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account or leave a group. Leaving a group stops it from seeing your status. Deleting your account removes your online data immediately.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open LastUp → tap the gear (Settings) → Delete my account → Delete forever. This removes your name, picture, uploaded photo, group memberships and morning history from our servers, clears everything on your phone, and turns off your alarm. If you were a group's Chief, the role passes to another member first.

Without the app: email avinashinduri7883@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my LastUp account" and the name you used in your group. We'll delete your data within 30 days.

Children

LastUp is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
